Train, run, evaluate and save Granite and other LLMs 2x faster with 70% less VRAM.

## Why Unsloth?

- Unsloth streamlines model training locally and on Colab/Kaggle, covering loading, quantization, training, evaluation, saving, exporting, and integration with inference engines like Ollama, llama.cpp, and vLLM.

- A key strength is our direct collaboration with teams behind Qwen3, Llama 4, Mistral, Google (Gemma 1–3) and Phi-3/4, where we’ve fixed critical bugs in their models that greatly improved model accuracy.

- Unsloth is the only training framework to support all model types: vision, text-to-speech (TTS), BERT, reinforcement learning (RL) while remaining highly customizable with flexible chat templates, dataset formatting, and ready-to-use notebooks.

## Key Features

- Supports full-finetuning, pretraining, 4-bit, 16-bit and 8-bit training.

- The most efficient library for RL, using 80% less VRAM. Supports GRPO, GSPO, DAPO etc.

- Supports all models including TTS, multimodal and BERT. Any model that works in transformers works in Unsloth.

- 0% loss in accuracy - no approximation methods - all exact.

- MultiGPU is in the works and soon to come!

- Unsloth supports Linux, Windows, Google Colab, Kaggle, NVIDIA and AMD & Intel setups.
